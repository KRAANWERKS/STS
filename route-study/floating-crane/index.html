05 / Operational assessment

Shorter ≠ tow-safe. The southern Combol/Dempo option measures shorter in this example, but the narrow exits must be assessed against crane beam, towline geometry, windage, under-keel clearance and tug control.

Offshore passage. Verify shoals, traffic, currents and seasonal wave conditions across Karimata Strait / Java Sea, then plan clearance south of Pulau Laut before the Makassar Strait turn.

Arrival. The end marker is a published pilot reference. Confirm actual deployment site, towing clearance, approach procedure, anchorage and pilot requirement with Balikpapan port/VTS.

Approval gates: current official ENCs + Notices to Mariners; bathymetry and tidal window; tug bollard pull and endurance; stability/sea-fastening/boom stow; towing survey and MWS clearance; BMKG metocean forecast; local port authorization.
